1. Executive Summary

MoodMark is a minimal, gas-efficient decentralized application that establishes an open on-chain primitive for personal sentiment and emotional status. By anchoring daily moods directly into a verifiable Solidity smart contract on Botchain, MoodMark eliminates centralized database reliance, algorithmically manipulated feeds, and third-party tracking.

2. The Problem

  • Centralized Exploitation: Traditional social applications hoard personal emotional records to fuel algorithmic recommendation loops and intrusive ad targeting.
  • Lack of Social Primitives in Web3: While DeFi and NFT primitives thrive, Web3 lacks simple, trustless indicators for real-time human morale and user status.
  • Fragility & Censorship: Off-chain mood trackers risk data loss, account freezing, and platform shutdown.

4. Smart Contract Specification

The core contract (MoodMark.sol) is deployed with maximum gas efficiency:

  • Storage Packing: The enum Mood (1 byte) and bool hasMood (1 byte) fit within a single 32-byte storage slot per user.
  • Custom Errors: Replaces expensive string reverts with custom error InvalidMood().
  • Event Indexing: Emits MoodUpdated(address indexed user, Mood mood) for zero-cost external indexers and explorer tracking.
